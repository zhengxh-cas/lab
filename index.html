What a biofilm is, how it forms, and why it matters

We study biofilms, microbial communities that are ubiquitous in nature. Our research focuses on how individual bacterial cells make physiological decisions in response to the environment within heterogeneous communities and how these decisions shape biofilm formation, virulence, and antibiotic resistance. We use cutting-edge multidisciplinary approaches to uncover the molecular mechanisms underlying these behaviors. Ultimately, our goal is to uncover fundamental principles governing bacterial biofilms and translate these discoveries into new strategies to address biofilm-associated challenges in medicine and industry.
